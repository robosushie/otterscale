Open-source mesh control

Team mesh without a per-seat lock-in.

You do not need a custom VPN app. You do not need to hand-write HuJSON for every hire. Otterscale is the console in front of Headscale — workspaces, roles, keys, and Network Apps — with the Tailscale clients people already run.
